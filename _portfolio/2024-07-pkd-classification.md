**Role.** Team Member &nbsp; · &nbsp; **Duration.** Jul 2024 – Dec 2024 &nbsp; · &nbsp; **Setting.** Industry–academia collaboration

**Goal.** Develop AI methods to distinguish PKD subtypes — *PKD1_Truncated*, *PKD1_non_Truncated*, *PKD2*, and others — from medical images, to support clinical decision-making.

**What I did.**
- Designed and implemented the **data preprocessing pipeline** for the medical image dataset.
- Applied deep learning for **kidney region segmentation** and downstream **subtype classification**.
- Iterated on architectures and training recipes to handle class imbalance and noisy labels typical of clinical data.

**Takeaways.** First exposure to working with medical imaging data under collaborative academic–industry constraints; learned how preprocessing decisions can dominate model quality in the medical domain.

**Stack.** Python · PyTorch · scikit-learn · NumPy · medical image processing libraries.
