**Role.** Team Member &nbsp; · &nbsp; **Duration.** Jul 2023 – Aug 2023 &nbsp; · &nbsp; **Program.** First SIG project at KLE Lab

**What I did.**
- **Crawled and preprocessed** movie plot data from Naver Series On to build a clean recommendation dataset.
- Implemented a **keyword-based recommendation engine** combining **TF-IDF** with **cosine similarity**, and learned **Word2Vec** embeddings for semantic keyword similarity.
- Learned the fundamentals of word embeddings and classical recommendation algorithms from the ground up.

**Stack.** Python · scikit-learn · gensim (Word2Vec) · BeautifulSoup / requests.
