  2. JDCS
    Feature-Based Beauty Product Search System through Feature Extraction from Product Reviews
    Tae-Wan Kim, Soo-Hyeon Park, and Hyunah Lee
    Journal of Digital Contents Society, Jun 2025
    Paper

    Reviews significantly influence purchase decisions for beauty products, where preferences vary by skin type. This paper proposes a system that uses KoBERT to extract key product features from reviews and visualize sentiment polarity, enabling feature-based product search. A demo of the deployed service is available at https://revieweyes.ajb.kr/.
