Hello! I am Taewan Kim (김태완), a final-year B.S. student in Computer Software Engineering at Kumoh National Institute of Technology, graduating in February 2026 with top honors (GPA 4.30 / 4.5).

Since 2023 I have worked as an undergraduate researcher across two labs — the Knowledge and Language Engineering Lab (KLE) and the Extended Reality & Metaverse Lab (XRM) — where I have published a KCI journal paper, filed two patents, and shipped a deployed review-search service (revieweyes.ajb.kr).

My research sits at the intersection of natural language processing, large language models, and multimodal AI. I am especially interested in:

  • LLM fine-tuning and prompt engineering — building domain-specific assistants (financial news, medical, beauty) using LoRA, instruction tuning, and structured prompting.
  • Retrieval and information extraction — fine-tuning encoders such as KoBERT for sentiment and aspect extraction, combined with Elasticsearch-based retrieval.
  • Multimodal and embodied applications of LLMs — leveraging LLMs for user interaction in VR/AR environments and for computer-vision-based scene understanding (traffic accident analysis).

In short, I like turning messy real-world text and images into something a model can usefully reason over.

  1. JDCS
    Feature-based Beauty Product Search System through Feature Extraction from Reviews
    Taewan Kim and others
    Journal of Digital Contents Society. Related patent: KR Application No. 10-2025-00178132. , Jun 2025
    Abs Bib HTML

    Korean cosmetic reviews contain rich, free-form descriptions of how a product actually feels and performs — texture, scent, suitability for specific skin types — but standard product search ignores most of this signal. We present a review-grounded search pipeline that fine-tunes KoBERT for aspect-level sentiment analysis on Korean cosmetic reviews, extracts product-level features (e.g., moisturizing, long-lasting, suitable for oily skin), and serves them through an Elasticsearch-backed retrieval engine. The system is deployed as a production service at revieweyes.ajb.kr.
