**Role.** Team Member &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Duration.** Jan 2024 – Feb 2024 &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Program.** Second SIG project at KLE Lab

### What I did
- Used the **Korean Dialogue Summarization Dataset** from AI HUB to train and evaluate summarization models.
- Studied and implemented several deep-learning-based summarization architectures, building foundational understanding of seq2seq and transformer-based models for NLP.
- Integrated the trained model into a **Discord chatbot** so users could request summaries of their chat history in-channel.
- Evaluated quality with multiple metrics (e.g., ROUGE family) and iterated on the pipeline.
