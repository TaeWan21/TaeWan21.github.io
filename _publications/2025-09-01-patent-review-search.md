**Scope.** A method and server architecture for serving search results that are grounded in user reviews rather than only product metadata. Closely related to the [Feature-based Beauty Product Search System](/publication/2025-06-cosmetic-feature-search) paper and the deployed [revieweyes.ajb.kr](https://revieweyes.ajb.kr/) service.
