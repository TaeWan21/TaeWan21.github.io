Served as a course mentor for **Data Structures** during the Spring 2024 semester at Kumoh National Institute of Technology.

- Held weekly mentoring sessions covering arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, hash tables, and graphs.
- Helped students debug C/C++ implementations of standard data structures and walk through the time/space complexity of their solutions.
- Designed supplementary practice problems oriented toward students who were seeing data structures for the first time.
